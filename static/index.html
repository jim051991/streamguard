Worldwide Legal Disclaimers & International Safe Harbor

1. Multi-Jurisdictional Safe Harbor & Intermediary Immunity

StreamGuard operates globally as an intermediary media player and indexing directory under international safe harbor doctrines:

  • United States: Title II of the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512) and Section 230 of the Communications Decency Act (47 U.S.C. § 230).
  • European Union & UK: The EU Digital Services Act (DSA - Regulation (EU) 2022/2065), Articles 4-6, Directive 2000/31/EC ("Mere Conduit" and "Hosting" liability exemptions), and the UK Electronic Commerce Regulations 2002.
  • India: Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000 read with the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.
  • Canada: Sections 41.25 and 41.26 of the Copyright Act (Notice-and-Notice Regime).
  • Australia: Part V, Division 2AA of the Copyright Act 1968 (Safe Harbour Scheme).
  • Singapore: Part 6, Division 2 of the Singapore Copyright Act 2021 (Network Service Provider Safe Harbours).

StreamGuard maintains an active, universal notice-and-takedown mechanism across all international jurisdictions.

2. Content Non-Hosting & Berne Convention Compliance

Zero Media Hosting: StreamGuard does NOT host, upload, record, capture, or store any proprietary video or television files on its servers. All live television channels indexed on this site are unencrypted Free-to-Air (FTA) web broadcasts distributed openly by their respective networks over the public Internet.

International Copyright Treaties: StreamGuard respects the Berne Convention for the Protection of Literary and Artistic Works and the WIPO Copyright Treaty (WCT). This platform functions strictly as an interactive playback client and indexing gateway.

Trademarks & Logos: All network names, logos, and trademarks (e.g., NASA, Deutsche Welle, Bloomberg, Red Bull, TRT World, CGTN) are the exclusive property of their respective trademark holders. Reference to any commercial entity does not imply affiliation, sponsorship, or endorsement.

Creative Commons: All on-demand open movies are officially licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY) courtesy of the Blender Foundation.

3. Cross-Border Streaming & Geoblocking Policy

Territorial Licensing Acknowledgment: Broadcasters distribute content subject to territorial broadcast licenses and international spectrum allocations governed by the International Telecommunication Union (ITU).

No DRM Circumvention: StreamGuard complies with Section 1201 of the US DMCA, Article 6 of the EU Copyright Directive 2001/29/EC, and international anti-circumvention treaties. This platform DOES NOT decrypt, crack, or bypass Digital Rights Management (Widevine, PlayReady, FairPlay) or paywalled subscription streams.

End-User Responsibility: Users accessing broadcast streams across international borders are solely responsible for ensuring that viewing complies with local laws, regional broadcast regulations, and television reception licensing laws in their jurisdiction.

4. Cybersecurity Telemetry & GDPR Recital 49 Notice

Worldwide Cybersecurity Monitoring: To ensure platform availability, prevent cyber warfare attacks, and defend infrastructure from automated exploitation, this platform deploys autonomous threat detection, honeypot canary traps, and IP geolocation telemetry.

GDPR / European Privacy Compliance: In full compliance with Article 6(1)(f) and Recital 49 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the processing of visitor IP addresses and threat signatures is legally recognized as a "Legitimate Interest of the controller in network and information security" for preventing unauthorized access, malicious bot floods, and denial of service.
